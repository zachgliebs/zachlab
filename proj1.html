FTC Robotics

My experience with FTC Robotics during my Senior Year of Highschool

Blender Onshape Large team collaboration

I made this 3D animation in Blender for the FTC World Championship that showcases the assembly of our robot.

Role CAD Designer, 3D Animation Lead, Quality Control
Focus FTC Robotics Competitions
Result Illinois FTC State 2025 Champion and World Championship Contender

Problem

  • Use my 3D modeling experience to help design a championship robot
  • Create animations to display at competitions to earn our team points
  • Calibrate the robot to make it as efficient as possible

Approach

  • Researched and learned 3D animation
  • Built a fully working model of each component of the robot
  • Created 3 different assembly animations of the robot
  • Spent many hours calibrating the robot with the drive team

A full render of the robot in Blender

Key outcomes

  • Learned a new 3D Modeling software and 3D Design program
  • Helped the build team understand the quickest way to repair and fix the robot
  • Inspired other teams to create similar animations in the future

Impact

Helped lead our team to a 1st place finish at the 2025 Illinois FTC Championship.
